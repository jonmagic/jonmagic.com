Clawdia is a private, single-user personal assistant I operate for my own use. It is not a public product, does not accept signups, and is not available to other users.

Its Gmail integration can:

- search messages in my mailbox
- read a message I select
- create a draft for me to review

Clawdia cannot send email through this integration. I review and send drafts myself.

Read the [privacy policy](/clawdia/privacy/) and [terms of use](/clawdia/terms/).
